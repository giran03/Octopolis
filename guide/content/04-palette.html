The command palette

One box for folders, files, commands, tabs, pins, shell commands and quick maths. Open it with , or jump straight to commands with .

The demo types through the modes below. Click a mode to try it.

Modes

The first character you type picks the mode. Without one, you are in Go to.

PrefixModeWhat it does
noneGo toFolders you use often and recently, pinned folders, places and drives; files and folders in the current folder; a typed path; a calculator.

Go to

  • Folders are ranked by fuzzy match and by how often and recently you used them. A row shows when you last visited, and drives show their free space. The empty palette lists Pinned, Recent, Drives and Recent commands.
  • Files in the current folder match too: Enter opens them, Ctrl Enter selects them in the list.
  • Paths: type C:\Us and the folders under C:\ are offered; Tab completes. ~ and %VARIABLES% work, and pasting a file's path opens its folder and selects the file.

Calculator and unit converter

Type a sum or a conversion and the answer appears at the top; Enter copies it.

12*8            → 96
(2+3)^2 / 5     → 5
sqrt(144) + pi  → 15.14159…
5 mb in kb      → 5,120 KB
2 h in min      → 120 min
100 c to f      → 212 °F

Sizes follow Windows (1 KB is 1,024 bytes). Lengths (mm, cm, m, km, in, ft, yd, mi), weights (mg, g, kg, oz, lb), times (ms to weeks) and temperatures (C, F, K) are known. A plain number such as a year is never mistaken for a sum.

Commands

Every command in Octopolis is here, written as Category: Title, with its shortcut. Commands you use rise to the top and a Recent group appears when the box is empty. When something is selected, the commands that act on it come first; when nothing is, they are shown muted with the reason ("Select something first") instead of disappearing. Your own custom actions are listed too.
