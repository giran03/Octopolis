Opening several files at once is a single batch: text files go to your editor in one launch, the rest open together, more than 10 asks first, and at most 50 open.

The transfer queue

  • Copies, moves, compression and deletes wait in a queue with a progress tray and a Cancel button. They can be paused and resumed, verified with checksums (BLAKE3) and limited to a chosen speed.
  • Taskbar progress follows the queue, the PC stays awake while files move, and a Windows notification tells you when an operation that took over 10 seconds finishes while Octopolis is in the background. Both options are in Settings › General.
  • A list of recent file operations is one command away, and friendly messages explain access-denied and missing-folder situations.
  • Symbolic links and junctions are copied as links instead of failing, and very long paths (over 260 characters) copy, move and delete normally.

Recycle Bin

The Recycle Bin window searches deleted items, restores them, deletes them for good, or empties the bin.

Batch rename

Select several items and press F2. Build names from patterns with numbering, dates and photo or music tags; use find and replace with regular expressions; or edit the list of names directly. A live preview flags conflicts before anything is renamed, and undo puts the names back.

Compress and extract

Compress to .zip, .7z or .tar.gz with progress and cancel. You can also browse inside zip, 7z and tar archives like folders, preview files in them, and copy items out.

OneDrive and the cloud

OneDrive files show whether they are online only or kept on this device. Use Always keep on this device and Free up space. Thumbnails and previews never download online-only files.
