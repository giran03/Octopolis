Views and Auto view

Show a folder the way that suits what is in it. The View button in the toolbar has them all, and one slider moves smoothly between them.

Zoom and shortcuts

  • The zoom slider in the View menu moves through List, Details, Content and Tiles as steps, then through icon sizes (40 to 256 px) smoothly.

  • Icons, tiles and the list stretch their cells to share the whole width, so resizing the window never leaves an empty strip at the side.
  • Every view supports selecting by dragging a box, drag and drop (including dragging files out), drops on folders, grouping and the right-click menu.

Auto view

Auto is on by default. When you open a folder for the first time, Octopolis looks at what is in it and picks a view, once. It never changes the view under you afterwards.

A view you pick sticks.

Choose a view by hand and that folder keeps it. The Auto row in the View menu (or ) hands the folder back, and the menu says why Auto chose what it did. You can switch Auto off entirely in Settings › General.
