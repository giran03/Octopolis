What Octopolis cares about

  • Speed first. Folders stream in as they are read, lists are virtualized, and heavy work (thumbnails, sizes, search) happens off the interface thread.
  • The keyboard is a first-class citizen. Everything has a command, every command can have a shortcut, and an optional Vim layer is built in.
  • It never surprises your files. Deletes go to the Recycle Bin, copies are checked on request, conflicts are asked about, and most operations can be undone.
  • It looks like you want. {{themes}} themes, a theme creator, your own fonts and icon style, and densities from compact to roomy.
