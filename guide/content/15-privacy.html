Privacy and your data

Octopolis works on your files on your PC. It has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry.

When it uses the network

Only when you ask it to, and only for these:

  • Google Fonts, if you switch it on and pick one of its fonts. The font downloads once, and can be saved on the PC to work offline.
  • Update checks, which stay off until a release feed is set up.
  • Dropping media from a web page onto a folder, which downloads that one file. Links to this PC or to a private network are refused.

What is stored, and where

  • Settings (theme, shortcuts, per-folder views and sort, pinned folders, recent folders and the folders you visit most, tags, custom actions and themes) are saved on this PC. Settings › Advanced › Your data opens the settings folder, and backs them up or restores them.
  • Caches (folder sizes, search, thumbnails) are managed under Settings › Advanced › Cache management, where you can clear any or all of them.
  • Diagnostics log: startup times and errors, with file and folder names removed. It stays on the PC; Settings › Advanced › Diagnostics log opens its folder.
  • This guide remembers only the theme you pick in it, in your browser, and reads the theme the app shares with it.

Safety nets

  • Deleting goes to the Recycle Bin unless you use Shift Del, which asks first.
  • Free up space, folder rules, the duplicate finder and clean up all preview before they change anything, and send things to the Recycle Bin where they can.
  • Uninstalling undoes the File Explorer replacement and removes the Explorer menu entry. The uninstaller asks whether to remove saved fonts.
