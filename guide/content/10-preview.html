Previews and the inspector

Look inside files without opening anything.

Inspector

or Space shows or hides the inspector panel beside the list. It previews:

  • Code with syntax highlighting, and Markdown.
  • Images and SVG, PDF, video, and audio with a waveform.
  • 3D models (OBJ, STL) and hex dumps for binary files.
  • CSV, TSV and JSON Lines as a sortable table of up to 10,000 rows, with a switch to the raw text.
  • Word, PowerPoint, Excel and OpenDocument files as text.
  • Fonts with sample text at several sizes, every character, and an Install button.
  • Folders as thumbnails, and archives by listing what is inside.

Text previews show the encoding, line endings and line count, with Open in editor (). Choose the editor in Settings › General › Editor: an installed one (found automatically) or any program, and whether double-clicking text and code files uses it.

Quick look

fills the window with the preview. The arrow keys step through files, Del moves the current one to the Recycle Bin, and Esc closes it.

Pictures

  • An image viewer for full-size viewing.
  • Resize or convert pictures from the right-click menu.
  • The duplicate finder can also look for similar pictures.
